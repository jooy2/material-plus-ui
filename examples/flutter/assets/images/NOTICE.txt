Sample pictures
===============

The faces, photographs and the product mark the example app puts on screen.
They are stand-in content, the way an app's own pictures would be, and none of
it is part of the library.

Each file is a copy of the picture of the same name under `docs/public/samples`
in this repository: the portraits from `people/`, the photographs from
`photos/thumbs/` and the mark from `marks/`. That folder's README says where
they came from: resized WebP files of assets in sample-assets
(https://github.com/jooy2/sample-assets), which is released under the MIT
license with the same copyright as this repository. The portraits are of wholly
fictional people, and the photographs are generated and are not of a real place.
